## During a bar-by-bar replay, when do you write a trade in the log?

- [x] As soon as the rules call it, before you advance to the next bar.
- [ ] Once the trade has closed and you know the result.
- [ ] At the end of the day, for all the day's trades at once.

## Why can a profile of the full session spoil a replay?

- [ ] Profiles cannot be drawn on historical data.
- [x] It includes trading that had not happened yet at the bar you are on.
- [ ] It makes the chart too slow to scroll.

Reread: bar-by-bar-replay

## A losing trade looks obviously bad in hindsight, but the rules called it. What happens to it?

- [ ] It is removed from the log.
- [ ] It is replaced with a better trade from the same day.
- [x] It stays in the log.

## Why do 20 trades say very little about a setup?

- [x] Luck alone can move the measured win rate by around 20 percentage points either way.
- [ ] A backtest must have exactly 100 trades to count.
- [ ] Short samples always overstate the losses.

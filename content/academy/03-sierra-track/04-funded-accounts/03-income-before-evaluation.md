## The order

This Academy teaches one order, and it does not change:

1. **Income.** Money from a job or business, left over after your bills, that can pay recurring costs without hurting anything else.
2. **A finished backtest.** A written setup card and a log of at least around 100 trades, every trade the rules called.
3. **A sim record.** The same setup forward tested on live data, with its numbers set next to the backtest's.
4. **Only then, if you still want one, an evaluation.**

| Step | What it shows | What it costs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Income | You can pay for tools without pressure | Nothing extra |
| Backtest | Whether the rules held up on history | Your time |
| Sim record | Whether you can follow the rules in real time | Your time |
| Evaluation | Whether you can follow them under a firm's rules | Fees |

Each step checks something the next one depends on. Skipping one does not save time; it moves the test to a place where failing costs money.

## What an evaluation cannot fix

It is tempting to see an evaluation as a shortcut. It is not one, because it cannot supply anything the earlier steps are for.

- **It does not give you a setup.** Without tested rules, each fee buys another attempt with no reason to expect a different result.
- **It does not fix discipline.** It adds rules and pressure, and the drawdown and daily loss limits punish exactly the mistakes that pressure causes.
- **It does not replace income.** Payouts depend on rules, can be irregular, and can stop. Nothing about an evaluation makes it a salary.
- **It does not make losses free.** Fees are real money. Losing fees instead of losing an account balance is still losing money.

Money pressure makes all of this worse. Fees paid from savings or credit make each losing day feel larger, and that feeling pushes you toward breaking rules, which is how evaluations end.

## How this Academy is built around that order

The Academy's structure is the order, laid out as courses.

- **Foundations** explains how markets, auctions, the tools and risk work.
- **The Backtesting School** turns an idea into written rules, tests them bar by bar and proves them in simulation. It is designed to be done without paying for software.
- **The Sierra Chart track** comes after it, for members who have income to pay for a platform and data, and who have the records to show a tested setup.
- **This chapter comes last** in that track on purpose. Evaluations are the final optional step, never the first.

Nothing in this Academy requires you to buy anything. If you never take an evaluation, you have lost nothing by learning how they work.

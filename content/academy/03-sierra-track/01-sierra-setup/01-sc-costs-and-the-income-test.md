## Why this lesson comes first

Sierra Chart is a paid platform. Before you install anything, you should know what it will cost you each month, and whether you should be paying it at all yet. This lesson gives you a way to work out the first and a test for the second.

It gives no prices. Prices change, and the only numbers that matter are the ones on the providers' own sites on the day you check.

## Software packages and what each includes

Sierra Chart is sold as a subscription, in several **service packages**. The packages differ in which features they include and which data services come with them. The advanced trading and order-flow tools that later lessons in this track use may not be in every package.

Before you choose, check on Sierra Chart's own site:

- Which package includes the studies and tools you actually plan to use
- Whether that package includes data, or needs a separate data service
- How billing works, and how you can change or cancel

Pick the package for the tools you have a tested use for, not the ones that look impressive.

## Data feed cost

Real-time futures data usually costs money on top of the software, in two layers:

- **Exchange fees.** Exchanges such as the CME charge for real-time data, usually per exchange, per month. The fee depends heavily on whether you count as a **non-professional** or a **professional** user, and the exchange's agreements define which you are. Read those forms carefully and answer them honestly; getting your status wrong can be costly.
- **The feed itself.** Sierra Chart can take data from its own data service or through a broker or trading-service connection, and the choice changes both the cost and what is included, such as market depth and history.

## The income test

Here is the rule this Academy is built on:

**Paid tools come out of income, never savings or credit.**

Income here means money left over from your job or business after your bills are paid. It does not mean expected trading profits. The test has two parts:

1. **Could you pay the monthly total from spare income for a year** without it hurting anything else in your life?
2. **Have you finished the Backtesting School** for at least one setup: a setup card, a backtest log with a large enough sample and a sim record?

If either answer is no, stay with the Backtesting School for now. It is designed to be done without paying for software.

> [!warning] Costs come before profits
> Trading costs money before it makes any, and it may never make any. Futures carry a real risk of loss. Paying for tools with savings or credit adds pressure, and pressure makes following rules much harder.
